## 🌌 Overview

**Half-Life: Exiled** is an upcoming standalone fan-made horror mod powered by the legendary GoldSrc engine. Shifting the focus from fast-paced gunplay toward tense survival, resource scarcity, and spatial isolation, the project takes players into the uncharted twilight biomes of Xen.

<img width="1025" height="217" alt="In the aftermath of the Black Mesa Incident, young engineer Nick is accidentally displaced into an uncharted, hostile sector of Xen  Cut off from Earth and hunted by alien horrors, his only route " src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2864f199-c1f1-42f6-abde-80a918a4d02b" />


> *In the wake of the Black Mesa Incident, young engineer Nick is accidentally displaced into an uncharted, hostile sector of Xen. Cut off from Earth and hunted by alien horrors, his only route to survival lies through deciphering strange alien tech and finding a way home.*

---

## 🛠️ Development & Toolchain

* **Game Engine:** GoldSrc (Half-Life 1)
* **Level Design:** [J.A.C.K.](http://jack.hlfx.ru/) (Jackhammer Map Editor)
* **Model Compiling & Animation:** Crowbar / Blender
* **Audio Engineering:** Custom spatial ambiance & voice design
* **Version Control:** GitHub Desktop

---

## 📂 Project Structure

* `src_maps/` — Uncompiled map source files (`.rmf`, `.map`, `.jmf`)
* `assets/` — Raw sound libraries, 3D meshes, and texture sheets
* `game_dir/` — Standalone mod client files and testing builds
